# About LIMA

LIMA is developed at [CEA LIST](https://list.cea.fr/en/), in the Text and Image
Semantic Analysis laboratory (LASTI), and has been used in many research and
industrial projects for more than twenty years.

- [License](license.md): LIMA is free software under the MIT license.
- [Credits](credits.md): the people and the third-party software behind LIMA.

## Related repositories

| Repository | Content |
| --- | --- |
| [aymara/lima](https://github.com/aymara/lima) | LIMA itself, including this documentation |

## Contact

For questions, bug reports and suggestions, please open a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/aymara/lima/issues).

CEA LIST can help you integrate LIMA in your projects and develop the
resources and models you need. Contact the LIMA maintainer, Gaël de Chalendar
(`gael.de-chalendar` at `cea.fr`).
